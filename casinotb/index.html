The Challenge Casino, Peru's favorite cookie brand for nearly 30 years, had just launched a full packaging redesign. The next step was to keep that momentum alive — with a limited edition bold and exciting enough to get people talking, buying fast, and wanting to collect it.
The Solution Casino handed the pen to its own community, co-creating a limited flavor called Tokyo Bloom: cherry blossom, taro, and ginger. The packaging follows suit — a Godzilla-like icon, lanterns, torii gates, and origami cranes in bold red and pink, in a dense cartoon-pop style. Only a few weeks on shelves, built to be collected.
Detailed Overview Casino Tokyo Bloom is a limited edition co-created with the Casino Lovers community, fusing cherry blossom, taro, and ginger into a new flavor for the brand. Available for just a few weeks, it was designed to spark urgency and social buzz. The packaging leans into Asian iconography: a Godzilla-inspired figure, cherry blossoms, lanterns, a torii gate, a daruma doll, and an origami crane, paired with kanji tied to fun and sweetness. Intense red plays against cherry blossom pink for shelf impact, with a custom logotype marking the edition apart from the standard line. Rendered in an expressive cartoon-pop style with dense, detail-rich illustration, the design rewards a closer look — controlled chaos built to catch the eye and turn a cookie pack into something worth collecting.
